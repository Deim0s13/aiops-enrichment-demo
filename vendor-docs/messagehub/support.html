Support

How to reach MessageHub support.

Opening a case

Open cases in the Console under Help, then Support cases. Choose a priority: Priority 1 for a production service unable to deliver, Priority 2 for degraded delivery, Priority 3 for questions. Quote the client ID, the time the problem began and example request IDs from the X-Request-Id header.

Response times

Business plan: Priority 1 within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day; Priority 2 within 4 business hours. Standard plan: Priority 1 within 2 hours. Business plan customers can also call the Priority 1 line on 0800 555 0142.
