Status and incidents

How MessageHub announces incidents, and what changes for clients during one.

Status page

Incidents and maintenance are announced on the MessageHub status page, status.messagehub.example, by region and channel. Customers can subscribe to updates for their region. Check it first when many requests fail at once.

Degraded mode

To protect delivery for all customers during an incident, MessageHub may reduce every client's limit in a region to 50% (degraded mode). Responses in degraded mode carry the header X-MessageHub-Degraded: true, and the status page names the affected region. Limits return to normal when the incident is resolved; limit increases are not granted during degraded mode.

What to do during an incident

Pace sends to the reduced limit, honour Retry-After, and hold messages in your own queue. Do not open limit-increase requests; if the incident affects your production service, open a Priority 1 support case referencing the status page incident.
