MessageHub developer documentation

MessageHub delivers transactional messages (SMS, email and push) for its customers through one REST API. These pages cover sending at volume.

Where to start

  • Rate limits: how limits are set per client and what happens when you reach one.
  • Handling HTTP 429: how to retry without making throttling worse.
  • Requesting a higher limit: permanent and temporary increases.
  • Status and incidents: degraded mode and where incidents are announced.
  • Support: opening a case and its priorities.
