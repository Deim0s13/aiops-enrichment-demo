Requesting a higher limit

Organisation administrators can ask for a client's limit to be raised permanently, or for a set period.

Who can request a change

Only an organisation administrator of the customer's MessageHub account can request a limit change. Engineers without that role should ask their organisation's MessageHub account owner to raise it.

Permanent increase

In the Console, open Organisation, then Plans and limits, select the client ID and choose Request change. Give the current and requested limit, the peak rate you expect and why. Increases above the plan's maximum need a plan change. Permanent increases are reviewed within 2 business days.

Temporary increase

For a planned peak or a production incident, request a temporary increase of up to twice the current limit for up to 72 hours, from the same page. On the Business plan, temporary increases are approved within 4 business hours; during a production incident, open a Priority 1 support case as well and quote the client ID.

Before you ask

Check that the throttling is not caused by retries ignoring Retry-After or by a MessageHub incident: a higher limit does not help in either case. Provide the client ID, the time the 429 responses began, your request rate and the size of any backlog.
